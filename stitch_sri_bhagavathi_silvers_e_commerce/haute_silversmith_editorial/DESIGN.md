## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authentic, high-end digital flagship for luxury Indian silver jewellery, silverware, and artisanal pooja articles. The aesthetic blends classical South Asian ornamental heritage with contemporary luxury editorial minimalism. 

Targeted at connoisseurs, bridal patrons, festive gift seekers, and heritage collectors, the interface conveys trust, 92.5 hallmark authenticity, and tactile preciousness. The experience emphasizes quiet luxury: expansive breathing room, warm natural undertones, whisper-light hairline borders, and museum-grade typography. Every interaction evokes entering a private salon boutique—deliberate, reverent, and exquisitely finished.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from raw, polished sterling silver resting on warm silk muslin and understated temple architectural tones:

- **Surface & Canvas (`#F8F6F1`)**: A foundational warm ivory and soft alabaster that eliminates sterile clinical whites, lending a tactile parchment warmth.
- **Deep Charcoal Ink (`#1C1C1C`)**: The primary text, primary button, and strong contrast anchor, grounding editorial serif titles and structural dividers.
- **Antique Champagne Gold (`#C5A880`)**: An ornamental metallic accent applied sparingly to hallmarks, purity badges (e.g., 925 Sterling), verified seals, and delicate flourishes.
- **Sterling Silver (`#B8B8B8`) & Muted Silver Fog (`#E8E8E8`)**: Used for fine structural hair-lines, framing borders, icon strokes, and subtle inactive track states.
- **Pure Highlight White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Reserved specifically for product showcase cards, floating modals, and elevated luxury trays to illuminate silver photography without cast reflections.

## Typography

The typography system relies on an interplay between literary serif grandeur and razor-sharp, functional sans-serif elements. 

- **Headlines & Narrative (Playfair Display)**: Used for editorial statements, collection names, section intros, and pricing numerals. Currency symbols and numeric pricing utilize tabular alignment with high-contrast serif figures to reflect high jewelry craft.
- **Body & Product Information (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Highly legible, neutral, and refined. Used for technical purity descriptions, gram weights, assay specifications, and long-form provenance stories.
- **Micro-Copy & Navigation**: Set in tracked, uppercase sans-serif (`0.14em` to `0.18em` letter spacing) to instill a timeless haute-couture atmosphere across utility links, filter groups, and category tabs.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure mirrors the pacing of an upscale art monograph: wide margins, deliberate horizontal alignment, and controlled editorial rhythm.

- **Grid Strategy**: A 12-column responsive layout anchored to a maximum content container width of `1440px`. Mobile viewports utilize 4 columns with `1rem` gutters and `1.25rem` screen margins. Tablet expands to 8 columns with `1.5rem` gutters. Desktop scales to 12 columns with `2rem` gutters and `3rem` to `5rem` outer margins.
- **Vertical Breathing Room**: Between major thematic sections (e.g., Hero to Heritage Curator, or Product Grids to Physical Flagship), deploy substantial padding (`4rem` to `6rem` desktop, `3rem` mobile) to avoid commercial clutter.
- **Asymmetric Feature Arrangements**: Curated collections and artisanal deep dives alternate between 7:5 and 6:6 split layouts, allowing full-bleed metal reflection imagery to balance refined technical specs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not rely on heavy drop shadows or aggressive floating surfaces. Instead, depth is achieved through **low-contrast outlines, tonal framing, and gossamer ambient diffusion**:

- **Hairline Framing**: Structural containers, category quadrants, and product tiles use a crisp `1px` border in `#E8E8E8` or `#E0DCD3`, establishing clear spatial order without heavy visual weight.
- **Ambient Silver Diffusion**: Elevated surfaces (quick-view modals, cart flyouts, sticky boutique headers) utilize a delicate, wide-radius shadow tinted with warm charcoal: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.05), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(28, 28, 28, 0.02)`.
- **Layered Warmth**: Background surfaces step smoothly from `#F8F6F1` (base canvas) up to `#FFFFFF` (isolated display cards and dialogs), offering organic, natural depth without stark contrast.

## Shapes

The design system intentionally adheres to **Level 0 (Sharp)** corner treatments. 

Sharp, rectilinear architecture reflects traditional silversmithing hallmarks, ingot ingresses, jewel display cases, and classical South Indian stone-carved plinths. Crisp 90-degree corners convey authority, structural precision, and architectural permanence. 

- **Exceptions**: Round elements are exclusively reserved for functional utility dots (color swatches, carousel indicator pips, notification count badges) and specific artisanal circular seals (such as BIS Hallmarking medallions). All cards, interactive buttons, modal frames, inputs, and image containers maintain disciplined, unrounded geometry.

## Components

### 1. Announcement Bar
- **Style**: Ultra-slim (`38px` height), background in `#1C1C1C`, text in `#F8F6F1`.
- **Content**: Subtle sliding or cross-fading announcements (e.g., "Complimentary Insured Pan-India Shipping on Silverware Above ₹5,000", "Certified 92.5 BIS Hallmarked Craft").
- **Typography**: `label-nav`, uppercase, with central text and auxiliary options for currency/purity consultation links.

### 2. Luxury Boutique Header
- **Layout**: Three-tier editorial or balanced dual-row structure.
  - *Top Tier*: Quick store locator, hallmarking credentials, concierge assistance.
  - *Center*: Primary typographic insignia (e.g., "SRI BHAGAVATHI SILVERS" flanked by subtle silver filigree or classical emblems).
  - *Action Bar*: Clean icon suite (Search, Account, Wishlist with counter badge, Shopping Bag) alongside uppercase editorial navigation categories (*Antique Temple Jewellery, Silver Utensils & Dining, Pooja Idols & Articles, Gifting, Silver Coins*).
- **Behavior**: Sticky state switches to pure white backdrop with a `1px` bottom border of `#E8E8E8` and micro-ambient drop shadow.

### 3. Editorial Hero
- **Structure**: Asymmetrical split or immersive centered presentation featuring high-resolution photography of antique patina and high-polish silver.
- **Elements**: Soft kicker tag in antique gold (`#C5A880`), major display headline in Playfair Display, editorial synopsis paragraph, paired with sharp dual CTA buttons: "Explore Collection" (solid black) and "Book a Virtual Video Tour" (ghost border).

### 4. Category Showcases
- **Geometry**: Architectural rectangular portrait frames (`3:4` or `4:5` ratio).
- **Details**: Inset thin border lines (`8px` within the outer boundary), subtle monochrome-to-warm zoom transition on hover (`scale(1.03)` with `transition: transform 0.6s cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), and anchored title cards set against `#FFFFFF` or semi-sheer alabaster backing.

### 5. Product Cards
- **Visuals**: Clean `#FFFFFF` image plinth with ample border padding around the silver artifact.
- **Information Hierarchy**:
  1. *Badges (Top)*: Crisp rectangular tags in `#F8F6F1` with `#1C1C1C` text for "92.5 SILVER", or `#C5A880` bordered tag for "ANTIQUE FINISH".
  2. *Product Title*: Playfair Display, 1-2 lines, balanced vertical rhythm.
  3. *Craft Details*: Small sans-serif caption denoting approximate net silver weight (e.g., `Net Wt: 48.50g`).
  4. *Pricing*: Playfair Display tabular numerals showing standard price and making-charge transparency.
  5. *Hover Interaction*: Revealing a subtle "Quick View" action line and a direct "WhatsApp Boutique Enquiry" button.

### 6. WhatsApp Concierge & Enquiry Buttons
- **Purpose**: Essential for high-value traditional Indian jewellery where customers verify weight, customization, and auspicious dates.
- **Design**: Refined outline button incorporating a delicate WhatsApp glyph, framed in deep charcoal or dark antique moss tone rather than jarring neon green, keeping the luxury aesthetic intact while remaining recognizable.

### 7. Trust Strips & Hallmarking Seals
- **Composition**: Multi-column banner with hairline dividers (`#E8E8E8`) highlighting:
  - *BIS 925 Hallmarked Purity* (Guaranteeing authenticated silver content)
  - *Transparent Weight Breakup* (Metal weight vs. gemstone/enamel tare)
  - *Insured Doorstep Delivery* (Tamper-evident luxury packaging)
  - *Lifetime Exchange & Buyback Policy*

### 8. Customer Reviews & Legacy Testimonials
- **Style**: Editorial quotes with classical quotation glyphs in `#C5A880`. 
- **Metadata**: Verified buyer name, city/region (e.g., "Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu"), and the specific silver heirloom or pooja article acquired.

### 9. Flagship Physical Store Locator
- **Layout**: Two-column split layout featuring boutique interior imagery alongside visiting hours, master silversmith consultations, Google Maps direction link, and direct concierge booking.

### 10. Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Button**: Solid `#1C1C1C` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `0px` radius, `0.14em` letter-spaced uppercase sans-serif, `14px 32px` padding. Transitions on hover to `#333333`.
- **Secondary Button**: `#FFFFFF` background, `1px` solid `#1C1C1C` border, `#1C1C1C` text. Inverts on hover.
- **Ornamental Accent Button**: `1px` solid `#C5A880`, `#1C1C1C` text, delicate champagne fill on hover (`#FBF8F3`).
- **Form Inputs**: Sharp rectangular fields, `#FFFFFF` fill, `1px` `#D6D6D6` border, focusing to `1px` `#1C1C1C` without harsh blue outlines.
